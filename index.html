IS THIS MY BALLOT?

Most races are the same for all of Princeton. A few depend on your address.

Races marked Depends on your address appear only on some Princeton ballots. Your voter registration card shows your voting precinct number, and the county's ballot lookup shows your exact ballot.

On every Princeton ballot

  • All statewide races and the 15th Court of Appeals
  • U.S. House District 3 (all of Princeton)
  • Fifth Court of Appeals, countywide offices, and county judges
  • Collin County Propositions A-D and Collin College Proposition A
  • Appraisal District board, Places 1-3

Depends on your address

  • Texas House: District 67 or District 89
  • Justice of the Peace: Precinct 2 for most of Princeton; Precinct 1 or 3 at the edges
  • County Commissioner: Precinct 2 race only south of U.S. 380; most of Princeton is in Precinct 3, which has no race this year
  • City Council: only inside Princeton city limits
  • School board: only inside Princeton ISD

HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Start with what the office can actually do.

Open any race to see the office's responsibilities, each candidate's background and stated reasons for running, and linked evidence. The guide does not summarize or compare candidates for you; each card uses the same fields so you can compare them side by side. Candidate priorities are labeled as candidate claims; official records and independent reporting are linked separately.

Important: This is not an official ballot and it does not recommend candidates or positions on propositions. It covers every item on Collin County's official all-race sample ballot that applies in Princeton, including county and Collin College bond propositions. Some races depend on your address, and each one is marked "Depends on your address." Check your official sample ballot for your precinct before voting.
