HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Start with what the office can actually do.

Open any race to see the office's responsibilities, a plain-English comparison, each candidate's background and stated reasons for running, and linked evidence. Candidate priorities are labeled as candidate claims; official records and independent reporting are linked separately.

Important: This is not an official ballot and it does not recommend candidates. The supplied personalized sample contained candidate contests but no ballot propositions. Check your official Collin County sample ballot for your precinct before voting.
