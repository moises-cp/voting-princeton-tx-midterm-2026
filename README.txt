Princeton & Collin County 2026 Voter Guide

Open index.html in a modern web browser. The guide works offline; an internet
connection is needed only when opening external source and campaign links.

Included ballot geography (all of Princeton, Collin County):
- Statewide races, U.S. House District 3, and Fifth/15th Courts of Appeals
- Collin County offices, judges, and propositions; Collin College; appraisal board
- Texas House Districts 67 and 89 (Princeton is split)
- Justice of the Peace Precincts 1, 2, and 3 (most of Princeton is in Precinct 2)
- County Commissioner Precinct 2 (south of U.S. 380 only)
- City of Princeton council races and Princeton ISD trustees

The website covers 53 contests, 97 candidates, and 5 ballot propositions, checked
against Collin County's official all-race sample ballot. Races that appear only
on some Princeton ballots are labeled "Depends on your address." It is a nonpartisan research
aid, not an official ballot. Confirm your precinct-specific official ballot and
voting location with Collin County before voting.

Research checked through October 18, 2026.
