Princeton & Collin County 2026 Voter Guide

Open index.html in a modern web browser. The guide works offline; an internet
connection is needed only when opening external source and campaign links.

Included ballot geography:
- City of Princeton
- Collin County
- U.S. House District 3
- Texas House District 89
- Justice of the Peace Precinct 2

The website covers 42 contests and 77 candidates. It is a nonpartisan research
aid, not an official ballot. Confirm your precinct-specific official ballot and
voting location with Collin County before voting.

Research checked through October 18, 2026.
